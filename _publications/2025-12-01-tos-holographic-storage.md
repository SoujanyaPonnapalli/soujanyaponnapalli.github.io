keyinsight: 'Holographic storage has long promised high density and fast random access, so in Project HSD we asked whether it finally makes sense for cloud storage. Using commodity optics, with machine learning correcting the noise they introduce, we reached 1.8 times the density of the previous state of the art and improved energy efficiency 14 times by optimizing media physics and background maintenance together. We conclude that it is still far from competing with hard drives and flash on density, capacity scaling, and energy efficiency.'
